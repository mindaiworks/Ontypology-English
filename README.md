From Difference & Similarity to Split & Merge, from Split & Merge to Type, from Type to understanding — the brain has walked this road for hundreds of millions of years.

A hundred years ago, logic was philosophy; once seen clearly, it became a prominent discipline (science).

Today, exploring "Type" (a concept of the brain) is philosophy. Ontypology takes "Type", sees it clearly, turns it into a prominent discipline (science), and puts it into practice as Mind AI.

Fei-Fei Li: The Digital Cambrian Explosion

She spoke of how, in the distant Cambrian era, everything was pitch black. A creature called the trilobite was the first to sense light; after sensing light, it developed a nervous system; what came next was "understanding and thinking", and finally execution. This is the law of biological development.
