From difference and similarity, to division and combination; from division and combination to form; from form to understanding — the brain has traveled this path for billions of years.

Logic was philosophy a century ago; once seen clearly, it became a manifest discipline (science).

Today, exploring "form" (a concept of the brain) is philosophy. Ontypology turns "form" into a clear, manifest discipline (science) and puts it into practice as Mind AI.

Ontypology = Ont (being) + Typology (types)

Fei-Fei Li: The Explosion of the Digital Cambrian

She describes how, in the distant Cambrian period, everything was pitch-black. A trilobite-like creature was the first to perceive light; after perceiving light, it developed a nervous system, followed by "understanding and thinking," and finally execution. This is the law of biological development.
